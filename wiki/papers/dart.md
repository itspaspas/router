# DART: Draft-Agreement Routing for Training-Free Adaptive Thinking Budgets in Hybrid Reasoning Models
arXiv:2606.23181 (Jun 2026). Category: [[reasoning-effort-routing]], [[quality-estimation]].

**Idea.** Sample two cheap *no-think* drafts. If they agree, return the direct answer. If they disagree, enable thinking with a budget set from draft entropy. No labels and no training.
**Evidence (authors).** Preserves or improves always-think accuracy in most settings, and cuts thinking tokens ~15–69% (math) and ~51–63% (code).
**Why it matters.** It is a concrete instance of "buy a cheap partial observation, then route", which is the core of [[gap-query-only-signal-ceiling]].
