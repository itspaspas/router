# RerouteGuard: Understanding and Mitigating Adversarial Risks for LLM Routing
Zhang et al. (Zhejiang U., Southeast U.), arXiv:2601.21380 (Jan 2026). Category: [[router-security]].

**Measurement.** Real-world routing systems are vulnerable to rerouting, especially *cost escalation*.
**Defense.** Embedding-based detection of adversarial prompts with adaptive thresholds. Reports >99% detection across three attack settings and four benchmarks with negligible impact on benign queries.
**Open.** Adaptive attackers who know the detector, and gadgets disguised as natural text ([[gap-adversarial-robustness]]).
