# RouterEval: A Comprehensive Benchmark for Routing LLMs to Explore Model-level Scaling Up in LLMs
Huang et al., arXiv:2503.10657, Findings of EMNLP 2025. Category: [[router-evaluation]].

**Contribution.** 200M+ performance records from 8,500+ LLMs on 12 evaluations. Finding: **model-level scaling-up**. With a capable router, performance keeps rising as candidates are added, so a router over many small models can rival large ones. Yet most existing routers leave significant room for improvement.
**Caveat.** Most of the 8,500 models are open-weight leaderboard models, and the records are single-draw.
