# Routing, Cascades, and User Choice for LLMs
Rafid Mahmood, arXiv:2602.09902, ICLR 2026. Category: [[economics-and-incentives]].

**Model.** A Stackelberg game between a provider (standard model and reasoning model) and a user who can re-prompt or abandon.
**Results.** The optimal routing is usually a **static policy without cascading**. Providers may have an incentive to **throttle latency**, which reduces user utility. The paper gives threshold rules for when routing, cascading and throttling help or hurt.
**Why it matters.** It is one of the few papers that model the *user* as strategic, and it questions cascade-heavy designs ([[cascades]], [[gap-transparency-and-control]]).
