# RouterArena: An Open Platform for Comprehensive Comparison of LLM Routers
Lu et al. (Rice), arXiv:2510.00202, ICLR 2026. Live board: routeworks.github.io. Category: [[router-evaluation]].

**Contribution.** A broad-domain dataset with difficulty levels, many metrics, and an *automatically updated leaderboard* that includes commercial routers.
**Initial board (figure-extracted, may have changed).** MIRT-BERT 67.3, Azure-Router 66.4, CARROT 63.9, vLLM-SR 63.8, GPT-5 router 62.7 (accuracy axis, plotted against cost).
**Significance.** It is the first place where academic and commercial routers are compared head to head ([[industry-landscape]]).
