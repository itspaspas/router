# Not Diamond
Commercial cross-provider router. Locus: third-party ([[industry-landscape]]).

- A predictive router trained to choose the best model per query across providers. It also offers custom routers trained on a customer's own evaluation data and prompt adaptation across models.
- Powers [[openrouter]]'s "Auto" model (per third-party directories).
- Vendor claims of large accuracy gains and cost cuts are not independently verified. [[llmrouterbench]] reports that commercial routers often fail to beat simple baselines on public benchmarks.
- Open-sourced "RoRF" (routing on random forests), a pairwise random-forest router (recalled; not re-verified this session).
