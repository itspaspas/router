# Martian
Commercial router company (founded 2022–23). Locus: third-party ([[industry-landscape]]).

- Markets "Model Mapping", described as using interpretability-style analysis of models to predict which model will do best on a prompt *without running it*, with claimed cost reductions of 20–97% (vendor claim).
- Authored and released [[routerbench]] (withmartian/routerbench), one of the field's foundational benchmarks.
- The claims connect to the white-box signal idea in [[prefill-activation-router]], but no peer-reviewed evidence for Model Mapping was found this session.
